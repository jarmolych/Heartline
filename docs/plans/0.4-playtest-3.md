# 0.4 plan — third playtest (2026-11-15, build 0.3.0-T3)

Source: the player's notes after playing `Heartline-0.3.0-T3`, plus two notes sent while the work
was under way. Each item says what was done and what is still open.

## 1. Progression between songs feels too easy
Player: "by song 2 I'm level 10 and everything feels like it hasn't gotten much harder."
- **Done** (`tune/playtest-3-gameplay`, `game.rs`):
  - Levels cost more: `XP_BASE + XP_LIN * level + XP_QUAD * level^2` shards (18, 35, 58, 87 ...
    instead of 14, 23, 32 ...). Level 10 now needs about 1650 shards instead of 545.
  - Pressure rises with depth (`RUN_ESCALATION` 0.07 → 0.14 per song), with the player's level
    (`LEVEL_PRESSURE`, 2 % each) and with rage (below).
  - The Heart's health scales with the build: `World::gun_power()` to the power
    `HEART_POWER_EXP` (0.8). Ordinary enemies gain 30 % health per depth.
- **Open:** a visible sign of depth beyond the banner, the modifier list and the new sky (for
  example a rim that grows hotter with every depth).

## 2. TEMPO upgrade makes the gun stop syncing
- **Done** (`level.rs::gun_track`): every TEMPO level now fires an even division of the beat
  (1, 2, 4 or 8 shots per beat, the power of two nearest its target interval, never slower than
  the level below). A shot leaves its grid slot only to land on a strong drum hit right next to
  it (`GUN_SYNC_FROM`). The old version walked the grid toward a target interval, which gave
  uneven gaps at the higher levels. Damage per second is still exact
  (`gun_dps_is_the_same_at_every_tempo`), and `gun_track_is_an_even_division_of_the_beat` pins
  the new behaviour.
- At some tempos two neighbouring TEMPO levels fire at the same rate; the higher one then hits
  harder instead. Not checked by ear.

## 3. Purgatory loops once you are strong
Player: the Heart dies, purgatory starts, the Heart respawns and dies again, and it repeats.
- **Done** (`game.rs`): breaking a phase early now enrages the Heart (`World::rage`, shown as
  RAGE I-V under the form name).
  - Everything left of the Heart grows by how much faster than planned the phase fell
    (`enter_purgatory`: `grow`, capped at 3x, scaled by `RAGE_KEEP_UP` 0.8).
  - Each rage step adds 10 % pressure (`RAGE_PRESSURE`).
  - Purgatory lasts 9 s at most (`PURGATORY_SECS`). If the section is still playing the Heart
    returns (`World::encore`), sized for the damage rate the player just showed.
  - Only the first two breaks of a song give a graded reward; later ones drop shards.
- `--sim ... --give 8 --song-idx 1` (a strong build on song 2): 1 to 3 early breaks per song.
- **Open:** whether this feels right is the player's call. A Heart that roams is still not built.

## 4. Ear candy
Player: be better at identifying ear candy (phone dials, chimes, cool little things) and add a
special touch to the level based on it.
- **Done** (`analysis.rs`): chimes are found from about 380 Hz up (`CHIME_LO`) and two-tone hits
  are recognised (`Chime::dual`). `find_candy` keeps only what a song does rarely:
  `CandyKind::Run` (four or more quick notes that move in pitch), `Dial` (two-tone hits) and
  `Rare` (a pitched sound used at most three times). A kind that turns up more than once per
  15 s is treated as part of the song's sound and dropped; at most one piece per 25 s is kept.
  `--analyze` prints them. Five Straftat songs gave 0 to 4 pieces each.
- **Done** (`level.rs` `Ev::Candy`, `game.rs`): each piece is marked "EAR CANDY" where the note
  sits in pitch. A run sends a comet across the arena that leaves a trail of shards
  (`Painter`); keypad tones lay out a 3 x 4 keypad of shards; a one-off sound releases a
  harmless golden sprite (`EnemyKind::Sprite`) that drops a heal when caught.
- **Open:** the 24-band spectrum is coarse, so this hears "a pitched flourish", not what the
  sound is. Telling a phone dial from a bell would need a finer transform around each hit.

## 5. Skies, props, transitions
Player: more skies, other changes between songs (palm trees for tropical songs, rain synced to
the song), the same idea in play, and a cooler transition between skies. Later: "those new sky
visuals are garbage (the moon one too)".
- **Done** (`art/playtest-3-visuals`, `render.rs` `BG_FRAG`): the skies were rebuilt after that
  note. Each backdrop has its own sky colours (`skyBase`) and a large centred body with shading
  and glow (`skyBody`): sunset (unchanged), moonrise, ocean dusk, wire globe, nebula, ringed
  planet, total eclipse, binary sunset, aurora. The small crescent moon, the vortex rings, the
  flat twin suns and the storm are gone.
- Horizon props (`skyProp`, `Mood::prop`): palm trees, pyramids, a city skyline whose windows
  flicker with the hats, meteors.
- Rain pulses with the hats, in the menu sky and in play. In play, calm sections drift with
  dust and drops bring rain if the song has no weather of its own; the floor style follows the
  phase palette.
- `level::mood` picks from families: warm mid-tempo songs get palms, ocean dusk and the binary
  sunset; dark dense songs get the eclipse, the globe and skylines; sparse songs get night
  skies. The song's hash picks within the family.
- Transition (`SKY_TRANSITION`, `u_tr`): when the look changes the old sky sinks behind the
  horizon, a band of light sweeps up through scan lines, and the new one rises.
- **Open:** tempo, brightness and drum density cannot tell that a song is "tropical"; the
  palms follow a rule of thumb. Skies under the arena in play are simpler versions.

## 6. Items, the Heart, the purgatory Heart
- **Done** (`render.rs`): upgrade orbs stand on a lit pedestal and carry the upgrade's own
  emblem (`upgrade_emblem`); shards are turning crystals that glint; heals pulse on the beat.
  The Heart's core goes dark while its shell is shut, the VALVE form has iris blades,
  OVERDRIVE burns and throws spikes on the kick, and the shell's edges shake loose as a phase
  runs down. In purgatory the Heart is a pale ghost: its edges drift apart around a dark void,
  embers circle it, a ring drains with the time left, and the floor fades.

## 7. Agents
Player: remove or reduce agents that did not help; does the playtester help?
- **Done** (`chore/trim-agents`): no agent was used in the sessions that built 0.3 and 0.4; the
  simulator, the analysis report and the screenshot self-test did the checking. `playtester`,
  `code-reviewer`, `gameplay-programmer` and `network-programmer` are removed. `planner`,
  `audio-dsp-engineer` and `graphics-programmer` stay for large separate jobs. `CLAUDE.md` says
  to work inline by default and lists the checking commands.

## Verified by machine only
37 unit tests; `--sim` on 6 Straftat songs with a 120 ms bot gave 2.1 / 2.0 / 3.3 / 5.0 / 5.8 /
6.5 / 8.3 hits per minute from LULLABY to FLATLINE; screenshots of every sky, the transition,
the items, the Heart forms and purgatory. Nothing here has been played or heard by a person.
