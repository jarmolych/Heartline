# 0.6 plan — menu layout, skies, foregrounds (2026-11-15, after build 0.3.0-T10)

Source: the player's notes on T10, sent with a screenshot of Risk of Rain 2's character select
as the model for the menu, plus notes added while the work was under way. Branch:
`feat/menu-ror2-layout`. Each item says what was done and what is still open.

## 1. Menu, in the player's words
- "For difficulty you scroll through it, and non selected ones are hidden as you scroll."
- "Different things are organized differently. Some things at the top, then a separator."
- "In the main menu the song is in the top right, so why do we move it to the middle and change
  it from Q and E to the menu buttons? Folder and file options should be moved closer to the
  track preview in top right."
- "I like the font in the screenshot, please opt for that instead of this pixelated font."
- "We need more icons and less flat text. Condense things more. Use softer edges."
- "Make sure menu UI can fit long names of Music Library or songs."
- "I dislike that it needs shift enter to do playlist, it feels a bit janky."

**Done** (`main.rs` `Screen::Setup`, `App::track_corner`, `ui.rs`):
- Run setup is three groups. Left panel: one tile per mode (like a roster), the mode's name and
  what it is, a line, then rows with a label cell (REMIX, SCORE, BEST). Right: the track
  preview, then a panel with a coloured DIFFICULTY header over a strip that scrolls, and a
  MUTATORS header over five icon tiles. Bottom centre: a big START. Bottom right: BACK.
- The difficulty strip (`ui::clip`, `App::diff_scroll`): the chosen tier sits in the middle,
  its neighbours either side, and the rest slide out of view and are cut off at the edge.
  Arrows at the ends, the mouse wheel, a click on a neighbour or LEFT / RIGHT scroll it.
- The track corner is one function used by the title menu and run setup, so the song stays top
  right with Q / E on both. Under the preview: previous, next, track list (T) on the left;
  play files, play a folder, live audio on the right; then a library bar with the library's
  name, its track count and two buttons (library from a folder, library from a playlist). No
  SHIFT anywhere. The title menu lost its MUSIC LIBRARY row and is in three groups with a line
  between them.
- Font: Oxanium SemiBold, built into the exe (`assets/fonts/oxanium/`, SIL Open Font License,
  downloaded from Google Fonts' GitHub repository with the player's go-ahead). It is the free
  font closest to the one in the screenshot (Bombardier, which is not ours to ship).
  `render::init_font` lets a `heartline-font.ttf` next to the game override it and falls back
  to Bahnschrift, then the pixel font. All text (menus and in play) goes through it. The file
  is the fixed-weight one from the repository's history: the current variable-font file draws
  at its thinnest weight in macroquad.
- Rounded corners everywhere (`ui::rrect`, `rrect_lines`, `panel`, `header`, `tile`,
  `go_button`), 23 new icons, key caps (`ui::keycap`) instead of hint sentences, and a results
  screen made of stat cells with icons and three buttons.
- Long names: `ui::marquee` slides a title that does not fit (track preview, library bar) and
  `ui::fit` cuts to a measured width (track list, records, in-play title).

**Open:**
- Whether Oxanium is close enough to the screenshot's font is the player's call; any TTF
  dropped next to the game as `heartline-font.ttf` replaces it.
- Options, Codex, Live Audio and the LAN screens got the font and round corners but not the
  panel layout.
- Hover and click were checked in screenshots only; nobody has used the new screens by hand.

## 2. Runs and songs
- "When you run out of songs, you should always be able to continue a run."
  **Done:** files and folders now follow the chosen mode (anything but SINGLE carries on into
  the library), the results screen always offers KEEP GOING unless the run was lost, and a
  track that comes round again is dealt differently (`App::track_seed`).
- "In menu it should go to the next song whenever your run ends."
  **Done** (`App::to_menu`): leaving a run or a Highway road moves the menu to the track after
  the one it ended on.
- "In the first 8 seconds of a new song playing in game, you should be allowed to press E or Q
  to switch the song." **Done** (`App::swap_song`, `SWAP_SECS`): E skips to the next track, Q
  goes to the library track before it; the build, score and depth stay. The HUD counts the
  window down.
- "Drop rush needs better phasing between songs, and when there is silence it should not at all
  completely change perspective. It looks super ugly."
  **Done** (`App::step_files`): the camera no longer leaves the arena for a silent stretch or
  between tracks; silence still clears the attacks once. In DROP RUSH the old track plays on
  past its section and the next one cross-fades in over it, coming in on a beat two bars before
  its drop. Live mode only drifts off after eight seconds without sound.
  **Open:** none of this was heard by a person; the cross-fade length (1.3 s) is a guess.

## 3. Skies, weather, foregrounds
- "Get rid of the spinning 3d globe orb menu sky." **Done:** backdrop 3 is now a striped
  crescent with a star in its bite.
- "Add more skies, weather effects, and add different foregrounds. One foreground I
  specifically want is waves of sound that genuinely look like an ocean."
  **Done** (`render.rs`, `level.rs` `Mood`):
  - Skies 11 to 13: signal (a core sending rings across the sky, lit by the kick), gate (a tall
    striped arch of light) and comet. 14 in all.
  - Weather 4 to 7: snow, storm (rain, cloud and a bolt on every kick), mist and wind. Motes
    and the aurora veil, which only showed in play, now show in the vista too.
  - `Mood::ground` picks the vista's foreground: 0 mountains, 1 the sea of sound
    (`draw_ocean`: 44 rows of waves rolling in from the horizon, the spectrum riding on the
    swell with the newest sound at the horizon, the bass lifting the surface, crests lit on the
    kick, a path of glints under the body in the sky), 2 the equaliser skyline (`draw_towers`:
    level-meter towers with a dark side wall), 3 dunes (`draw_dunes`). When the look changes
    the old ground sinks before the new one rises. Highway and Rally keep the grid floor.
  **Open:** the dunes are the weakest of the three; the comet's tail is a first version.

## Verified by machine only
39 unit tests; screenshots of every new screen at 16:9 and 4:3, of each ground, the new skies
and a sky transition; a two-song DROP RUSH run and a song swap in both directions without a
crash. Debug switches added for this: `--library PATH`, `--screen results`, `--swap 1|-1`, and a
fifth value (ground) on `--mood` / `--mood-to`.
