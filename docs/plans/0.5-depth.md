# 0.5 plan — depth pass (2026-11-15)

Source: the player, after `Heartline-0.3.0-T4`: fix several skies, then "focus on the current
plan and expand on it as necessary to genuinely improve the project": difficulty system, item
variety, visual variety, enemies, the Heart's mechanics, song-to-attack syncing, song-to-visual
syncing, side game modes, menu layout, and anything else worth doing.

This plan takes what earlier plans left open (see the "Open" notes in `0.3-playtest-2.md` and
`0.4-playtest-3.md`) and adds to it. Each section says what the game does today, what is wrong
with it, and what to build. Sections are ordered by how much they change how the game plays.
Every item ends in a passing build; progress is recorded under each heading.

## 0. Sky fixes the player asked for
Ringed planet, city, black-hole edges, palm trees, rain, and a "tidal waves" sky.
- **Done** (`art/sky-polish`): see the commit. Ten skies now; the eclipse became a black hole.

## 1. The Heart moves
- **Today:** the Heart sits at the centre for the whole run. Forms (0.3 slice 4) change what you
  shoot at, but you always shoot inward from wherever you like, so positioning hardly matters.
  The player's first complaint about it was "shooting a sitting Heart is boring".
- **Build:** the Heart relocates. On a bar line every few bars it glides to another anchor point
  over two beats, with its destination drawn first. Everything it does comes from where it is:
  pulses and their warning zones, darts, shockwaves, the Split cores, the collision. Calm
  sections (Shell) keep it at the centre so the shield nodes stay readable. It never moves in
  purgatory.
- **Why this and not a free-roaming Heart:** warnings must stay honest. Moving only on bar lines
  between known points keeps every telegraph readable and keeps the song in charge of when.
- Harder tiers move it more often; LULLABY rarely.

## 2. Attacks land on the beat, not just start on it
- **Today:** a pulse fires on a kick, but its bullets then travel at a speed that has nothing to
  do with the tempo, so they reach you at arbitrary times.
- **Build:** bullet speeds are chosen so a pulse crosses a fixed ring around the Heart (the
  "beat ring", 320 px out) a whole number of half-beats after it fires. The ring is drawn
  faintly and flashes as rings of bullets cross it, so you can read and feel when a wave will
  reach your lane. Pressure no longer changes bullet speed (it changes how many).
- **Graze:** passing close to a bullet without being hit gives score and combo. It rewards
  playing near the pattern instead of hiding at the rim, and gives the Dodge mode (section 7)
  its scoring.

## 3. Enemies
- **Today:** Chaser, Darter, Orbiter, Splitter (and Minis), Elite, Wisp. All of them either walk
  at you or circle; none controls space or punishes ignoring it on a musical clock.
- **Build two with their own rhythm:**
  - **Metronome** (turret): plants itself where it arrives and fires a three-shot fan at you on
    every downbeat, with a ring that fills through the bar so you can see it coming. Kill it or
    keep moving on the one.
  - **Cymbal** (bomber): drifts toward you; within range it arms, shows a ring, and bursts into
    a circle of bullets two beats later. Killing it first gives a bonus.
- Later: a shielded enemy that must be flanked; a "choir" that buffs others until shot.

## 4. Items
- **Today:** 10 common and 3 rare upgrades, nearly all flat stat steps.
- **Build six that change how you play:**
  - **ORBIT** (rare): blades circle you, cutting enemies and shootable notes.
  - **NOVA:** being hit clears every bullet near you.
  - **CRESCENDO:** damage grows with your combo multiplier.
  - **ACCENT:** a chance for a shot to land two and a half times as hard.
  - **CODA:** breaking a Heart phase or clearing a track heals you.
  - **SYNCOPE:** a dash started on the beat costs no cooldown.
- Each gets its own emblem. Later: items with a drawback (cursed picks), active items on a key.

## 5. Difficulty
- **Today:** seven tiers that scale budgets, warnings, ramp and mercy; depth adds a modifier per
  song. Nothing lets the player shape a run, and a hard tier scores the same as an easy one.
- **Build:**
  - **Mutators** chosen in run setup (like RoR2 artifacts): ALLEGRO, FORTE, SWARM, NO MERCY
    (today's depth modifiers) and GLASS (two lives, 60 % more damage). Depth still adds its own.
  - **Score multiplier:** each tier and each mutator multiplies the score, shown in setup.
  - **High scores** per track and tier, saved next to the exe, shown in setup and on results.
- Later: a difficulty that adapts within a tier to how the run is going; per-tier leader lists.

## 6. Song-to-visual sync
- **Today:** kick zoom and ripples, snare rings, hat sparkles, build-up and drop, sky bodies that
  breathe with the bass, windows and rain on the hats.
- **Build:** the beat ring (section 2); chimes light a star in the menu and drift sky where the
  note sits in pitch; ear candy sends a flash through the whole sky.
- Later: per-mood rim shapes and Heart solids; depth shown in the arena rim.

## 7. Side modes
- **Today:** Endless, Single, Daily seed, and LAN Rally.
- **Build:**
  - **DODGE:** no gun. The Heart cannot be hurt; you score by grazing and by surviving. A
    different game on the same songs, and a good way to learn patterns.
  - **DROP RUSH:** each track is cut down to its biggest section. Short, dense runs.
- Later: the beat platformer from the first design notes; co-op over the LAN layer.

## 8. Menu layout
- **Build:** run setup gains a MUTATORS row and the two new modes, shows the score multiplier and
  the best score for the chosen track and tier. Results show NEW BEST.
- Later: a logbook screen (enemies, items, songs played), key rebinding, gamepad.
