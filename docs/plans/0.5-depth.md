# 0.5 plan — depth pass (2026-11-15)

Source: the player, after `Heartline-0.3.0-T4`: fix several skies, then "focus on the current
plan and expand on it as necessary to genuinely improve the project": difficulty system, item
variety, visual variety, enemies, the Heart's mechanics, song-to-attack syncing, song-to-visual
syncing, side game modes, menu layout, and anything else worth doing.

This plan takes what earlier plans left open (see the "Open" notes in `0.3-playtest-2.md` and
`0.4-playtest-3.md`) and adds to it. Each section says what the game does today, what is wrong
with it, and what to build. Sections are ordered by how much they change how the game plays.
Every item ends in a passing build; progress is recorded under each heading.

## 0. Sky fixes the player asked for
Ringed planet, city, black-hole edges, palm trees, rain, and a "tidal waves" sky.
- **Done** (`art/sky-polish`): see the commit. Ten skies now; the eclipse became a black hole.

## 1. The Heart moves
- **Today:** the Heart sits at the centre for the whole run. Forms (0.3 slice 4) change what you
  shoot at, but you always shoot inward from wherever you like, so positioning hardly matters.
  The player's first complaint about it was "shooting a sitting Heart is boring".
- **Build:** the Heart relocates. On a bar line every few bars it glides to another anchor point
  over two beats, with its destination drawn first. Everything it does comes from where it is:
  pulses and their warning zones, darts, shockwaves, the Split cores, the collision. Calm
  sections (Shell) keep it at the centre so the shield nodes stay readable. It never moves in
  purgatory.
- **Why this and not a free-roaming Heart:** warnings must stay honest. Moving only on bar lines
  between known points keeps every telegraph readable and keeps the song in charge of when.
- Harder tiers move it more often; LULLABY rarely.

## 2. Attacks land on the beat, not just start on it
- **Today:** a pulse fires on a kick, but its bullets then travel at a speed that has nothing to
  do with the tempo, so they reach you at arbitrary times.
- **Build:** bullet speeds are chosen so a pulse crosses a fixed ring around the Heart (the
  "beat ring", 320 px out) a whole number of half-beats after it fires. The ring is drawn
  faintly and flashes as rings of bullets cross it, so you can read and feel when a wave will
  reach your lane. Pressure no longer changes bullet speed (it changes how many).
- **Graze:** passing close to a bullet without being hit gives score and combo. It rewards
  playing near the pattern instead of hiding at the rim, and gives the Dodge mode (section 7)
  its scoring.

## 3. Enemies
- **Today:** Chaser, Darter, Orbiter, Splitter (and Minis), Elite, Wisp. All of them either walk
  at you or circle; none controls space or punishes ignoring it on a musical clock.
- **Build two with their own rhythm:**
  - **Metronome** (turret): plants itself where it arrives and fires a three-shot fan at you on
    every downbeat, with a ring that fills through the bar so you can see it coming. Kill it or
    keep moving on the one.
  - **Cymbal** (bomber): drifts toward you; within range it arms, shows a ring, and bursts into
    a circle of bullets two beats later. Killing it first gives a bonus.
- Later: a shielded enemy that must be flanked; a "choir" that buffs others until shot.

## 4. Items
- **Today:** 10 common and 3 rare upgrades, nearly all flat stat steps.
- **Build six that change how you play:**
  - **ORBIT** (rare): blades circle you, cutting enemies and shootable notes.
  - **NOVA:** being hit clears every bullet near you.
  - **CRESCENDO:** damage grows with your combo multiplier.
  - **ACCENT:** a chance for a shot to land two and a half times as hard.
  - **CODA:** breaking a Heart phase or clearing a track heals you.
  - **SYNCOPE:** a dash started on the beat costs no cooldown.
- Each gets its own emblem. Later: items with a drawback (cursed picks), active items on a key.

## 5. Difficulty
- **Today:** seven tiers that scale budgets, warnings, ramp and mercy; depth adds a modifier per
  song. Nothing lets the player shape a run, and a hard tier scores the same as an easy one.
- **Build:**
  - **Mutators** chosen in run setup (like RoR2 artifacts): ALLEGRO, FORTE, SWARM, NO MERCY
    (today's depth modifiers) and GLASS (two lives, 60 % more damage). Depth still adds its own.
  - **Score multiplier:** each tier and each mutator multiplies the score, shown in setup.
  - **High scores** per track and tier, saved next to the exe, shown in setup and on results.
- Later: a difficulty that adapts within a tier to how the run is going; per-tier leader lists.

## 6. Song-to-visual sync
- **Today:** kick zoom and ripples, snare rings, hat sparkles, build-up and drop, sky bodies that
  breathe with the bass, windows and rain on the hats.
- **Build:** the beat ring (section 2); chimes light a star in the menu and drift sky where the
  note sits in pitch; ear candy sends a flash through the whole sky.
- Later: per-mood rim shapes and Heart solids; depth shown in the arena rim.

## 7. Side modes
- **Today:** Endless, Single, Daily seed, and LAN Rally.
- **Build:**
  - **DODGE:** no gun. The Heart cannot be hurt; you score by grazing and by surviving. A
    different game on the same songs, and a good way to learn patterns.
  - **DROP RUSH:** each track is cut down to its biggest section. Short, dense runs.
- Later: the beat platformer from the first design notes; co-op over the LAN layer.

## 8. Menu layout
- **Build:** run setup gains a MUTATORS row and the two new modes, shows the score multiplier and
  the best score for the chosen track and tier. Results show NEW BEST.
- Later: a logbook screen (enemies, items, songs played), key rebinding, gamepad.

## Order
0 (done) → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 + 8 → 7 → 6. After each: unit tests and `--sim`; screenshots for
anything visible. Balance numbers go in `PROJECT_STATE.md`.

## Progress (2026-11-15)
All eight sections have a first version, each on its own branch stacked on the last. Checked by
unit tests (38 pass), the balance simulator and screenshots; nothing here has been played.

- **0. Skies** (`art/sky-polish`). After the player's second note (no surface textures; the sun
  works because of its CRT bars, soft edge, pulse and motion) the moon and planet became flat
  gradients with drifting scan bars and bass-breathing halos. Pyramids were replaced by glowing
  lattice radio masts. City towers have a lit front, a dark side wall and outlines, and their
  windows are a level meter on the bass. Black hole, feather-frond palms, three-depth rain with
  splashes, and the tide sky are in `BG_FRAG` (`skyBase`, `skyBody`, `skyProp`, `rainLayer`).
- **1. The Heart moves** (`feat/heart-moves`). `Heart::pos`, `HEART_ANCHORS`,
  `World::maybe_move_heart` (decided on downbeats, at least `HEART_STAY_SECS` apart, from
  `HEART_MOVES_FROM` hardness up, so LULLABY and RESTING keep it still). It announces for a beat
  (`draw_heart_move`), glides over two, and holds its fire meanwhile. The renderer draws the
  Heart's layers around the origin and shifts them with `set_origin`.
  - Changed from the plan: anchors were pulled in to within 300 px of the centre. With the
    first, wider set the bot took twice as many hits because it was squeezed against the walls.
- **2. Beat-timed attacks** (`feat/beat-sync-graze`). `level::beat_speed` and `BEAT_RING_R`;
  the ring is drawn in `draw_heart_rings`. Graze: `GRAZE_R`, `Stats::grazes`.
- **3. Enemies** (`feat/enemies-items`). `EnemyKind::Turret` (Metronome) and `Bomber` (Cymbal);
  both act in `World::on_beat`.
- **4. Items** (same branch). ORBIT, NOVA, CRESCENDO, ACCENT, CODA, SYNCOPE; 19 upgrades in all.
- **5 + 8. Difficulty and menu** (`feat/mutators-modes-scores`). `World::base_mods`,
  `MOD_GLASS`, `score_mult`; `TIER_SCORE` and `MUTATOR_SCORE`; best scores in
  `heartline-scores.txt` (`score_key`, `record_score`). Run setup has the MUTATORS row, the
  score multiplier and the best score.
- **7. Side modes** (same branch). DROP RUSH (`MODE_RUSH`, `Song::end_t`) and DODGE
  (`World::pacifist`). Debug: `--mode N`, `--mutators BITS`.
- **6. Visual sync** (`art/chime-stars`). `u_fx`: a chime lights a star by pitch, ear candy
  shimmers the sky.

Balance (`--sim`, 6 Straftat songs, 120 ms bot, hits per minute, LULLABY to FLATLINE):

## Second pass (2026-11-15, while the player was away)
The player's brief: take the wheel; overhaul where that is better than adding; improve every
part of the game; LAN Rally need not be the only side mode; live mode needs work.

- **Live audio** (`feat/live-phases`): each section the live generator announces becomes a Heart
  phase on the spot (`World::live_phases`, `push_live_phase`), so live mode has forms, purgatory,
  rage, the moving Heart and beat-timed pulse speeds. The sky changes every third section.
  Not run against real audio here: there is nothing to capture in a test.
- **The Heart charges** (`feat/charge-resonance`): in OVERDRIVE it lights a lane through where
  the player stands and crosses it a beat later (`Heart::charging`, `CHARGE_EVERY_BEATS`).
- **Resonance** (same branch): a meter under the combo bar, filled by grazes, kills, on-beat
  dashes and damage to the Heart. Full, it rings for `RESONANCE_SECS`: 1.5x damage, double score,
  a wider magnet.
- **Highway** (`feat/highway-mode`, `src/runner.rs`): a second side mode on the title menu. A
  three-lane run down the grid in the menu vista's perspective. Kicks put a wall in a lane,
  snares lay a bar to jump, hats string gems, chimes drop stars; all arrive on their sound. It
  uses the tier chosen in PLAY, keeps a best score per track and tier, and has an autopilot
  (`Runner::autopilot`) that the unit test and `--screen highway` use.
- **LAN Rally** (`feat/rally-heat`): every four returns in a rally the orb's flight loses half a
  beat, down to one (`Rally::heat`, host side; the count is shown). The practice bot's error
  rate follows the difficulty tier (`Rally::bot_miss`).
- **Codex** (`feat/codex-finale`): a title-menu screen with five pages: the Heart, enemies, all
  19 upgrades with emblems, how the music becomes the level, and the best scores.
- **Finale** (same branch): in the last section of a song every pulse has an echo half a beat
  later, off to one side and shootable (`World::in_finale`).
- HUD: DODGE shows its own line instead of a Heart bar; the depth is named next to the tempo.

Checked: 39 unit tests, an 85-second automated run through a finale into the next song with 12
random upgrades (no crash, 144 fps), `--sim` after the charge (about 2.0 / 2.4 / 3.8 / 3.5 / 4.7
/ 5.9 / 7.2 hits per minute), screenshots of Highway and the Codex.

**Still open**
- Highway has one obstacle set and no sections of its own; it wants speed changes on drops, a
  second craft ability and its own results rank.
- Rally heat is not sent to the client, so only the host and practice see the count.
- Live mode has no layers or ear candy, and capturing a single app is still `live-audio.md`.
- The simulator's bot still ignores upgrades, mutators and modes.

- **Highway** (`feat/highway-depth`): surges and drops put the road in overdrive (double score,

## Fourth pass (2026-11-15, same sitting)
- **Carry a charge** (`feat/charge-carry`): while the Heart's body can be hurt (VALVE,
  OVERDRIVE) a charge appears far from it (`World::fork`). Carried into the ring around the
  Heart (`FORK_DELIVER_R`) it removes `FORK_SHARE` of the phase at once; a hit knocks it loose
  where the player stands. This is the "carry a charge" side objective from the first Heart
  proposal. Results now list grazes and phases broken early.
- **Milestones** (`feat/milestones`): twelve goals kept across runs in the scores file
  (`ACH|id` keys), announced when earned, listed on a sixth Codex page.
- **Live pulses are announced** (`feat/live-predict`): reactive live mode (no second output
  device) used to fire pulses the instant a kick was heard, with no warning. With a confident
  beat (`live_beat.conf > 0.4`) the app now predicts the next beat and announces its pulse a
  full warning ahead (`LiveGen::on_predicted_beat`, `make_pulse`); kicks stop triggering pulses
  while that holds. Not run against real audio here.

## Fifth pass (2026-11-15, same sitting)
- **Comb and Sweep** (`feat/comb-sweep`): two more aimed patterns in the decks for Normal and
  hot sections. Comb fans five short lines `COMB_STEP` apart around the player; Sweep is a cone
  whose far side leaves slower, so it swings across like a scythe, from alternating sides.
  Emission (`World::emit_pulse`), warning zones (`render.rs::pulse_dirs`) and the bot
  (`tools.rs`) all know them. Levels changed once as a result (the decks draw differently).
  `--sim`: 2.2 / 2.3 / 3.8 / 4.8 / 5.3 / 5.3 / 6.4 hits per minute.
- **Track list** (`feat/track-picker`): in run setup, clicking the starting track or pressing T
  opens the whole library as a list (`Screen::Tracks`): arrows, PageUp / PageDown, the wheel or
  a typed letter to move; the best score for the current tier and mode is shown per title.
- **Depth frames** (same branch): `draw_rim` adds one frame of danger-coloured light around the
  arena per song deep the run is, up to four. This closes the "visible sign of depth" item.

- **Thread** (`feat/thread`, the last change of the sitting): dashing straight through a bullet
  that would have hit counts as three grazes and feeds resonance (`Stats::threads`).
- The player's standing brief and art direction are now in `CLAUDE.md`.
- A crash sweep at the end ran DODGE, DROP RUSH across two songs, FLATLINE with every mutator,
  a 100-second Highway road, every Codex page: no crash.
