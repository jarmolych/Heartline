# 0.7 plan — the depth run (2026-11-15)

## Why
The player, after T13: "I don't really understand the point of the game, is it to live as long
as possible or to kill the heart? It kind of feels weird. I think you get punished in difficulty
for killing the heart too." All true of the game as it stood: the only way to lose was to die,
breaking the Heart was a side goal, and breaking a phase early raised the Heart's health and
the pressure (rage).

Their direction: "Maybe the goal is to get as deep as you can, and killing the heart gives you
purgatory + rewards for a while, and killing it earlier gives you more rewards. Earlier you kill
it, it sends you deeper than killing it later. So the game is harder because you're deeper, but
that is the overarching goal anyway. Killing the heart sooner should give you a better reward to
deepness ratio. If you keep getting stuck at depth 10, becoming better and killing the heart
faster can make you still viable to depth 12." And: "make damn sure that you are both rewarded
and that it won't be boring after."

## The rules
1. **The goal is depth.** DEPTH is on the HUD all the time, it is the headline of the results
   screen and what the records keep. Depth is the difficulty: each depth adds pressure, enemy
   health and Heart health, and every second depth switches on another modifier.
2. **Every Heart has a clock.** It is born at the start of the song and the song's end is its
   deadline. The clock is drawn under its health bar with two marks.
3. **Breaking the Heart is a dive.** How much of its clock was used decides how far:
   | Clock used | Dive | Depth | Reward |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | up to 50% | ABYSSAL | +3 | 3 offers (each with a rare), 2 free upgrades, full heal |
   | up to 75% | DEEP | +2 | 2 offers (the first with a rare), 1 free upgrade, 1 heart back |
   | later | DIVE | +1 | 1 offer |
   Rewards per depth: 1, 1.5, 1.67. A faster kill is deeper and better equipped for it.
4. **Then purgatory.** Ten seconds (twelve or fourteen after a bigger dive): everything hostile
   becomes shootable notes, enemies burst into shards, the offers are on the field.
5. **Then a new Heart, at the new depth,** with the rest of the song as its clock. It can be
   broken too, for another dive. If less than 35 seconds of the song are left there is no new
   Heart and the rest is a short victory lap.
6. **A song always takes you down at least one.** If its first Heart is never broken it
   "escapes": depth +1 and nothing gained. Later Hearts in the same song are a bonus; leaving
   one alive costs nothing.
7. **No rage, no phase floors.** The Heart has one pool of health. Its forms still follow the
   song's sections (Shell in calm parts, Valve, Split, Overdrive on drops), but nothing stops
   the health bar and nothing grows because you were fast. Breaking all of a Shell's or Split's
   nodes early leaves the Heart exposed until the section changes.
8. The offer at the end of every track is gone: a kill is the reward now. Offers in calm
   sections and level-ups from shards stay as the baseline everyone gets.

Live mode uses the same rules with a 210 second clock per Heart. DODGE has no kills: it sinks
one depth a song.

## Numbers (first pass, to be tuned with the simulator)
`DEPTH_PRESSURE` 0.10 per depth, enemy health +18% per depth, Heart health +10% per depth,
a modifier every 2 depths, elites' second and third kinds from depth 3 and 6. `HEART_HP_PER_SEC`
is tuned so the plain bot on PULSE breaks a song's Heart with most of its clock used.

## What else is planned for the gameplay (after this lands and is played)
In the order I would do them:
1. **Tune the dive.** The clock marks, the rewards and the per-depth slope are first guesses.
   They need the player's hands, then simulator runs at depth 6, 12 and 18.
2. **Deep Hearts.** From depth 6 and 12 the Heart gains a trait for that run (for example:
   mirrored pulses, a second Shell, it heals in calm sections), announced when it arrives, so
   depth 12 is a different fight and not only a faster one.
3. **A choice at each dive.** After a DEEP or ABYSSAL dive, pick one of two "currents" for the
   next stretch: a risk with a payoff (for example +1 depth now for a rare, or enemies drop
   double shards but bullets are faster). Gives the build a direction.
4. **Upgrade synergies.** Named pairs that do something extra together, shown in the codex, so
   offers are decisions and not just bigger numbers.
5. **An active ability** on full Resonance (a held burst or a short slow of time) so the meter
   is something you spend, not only a buff.
6. **Enemies that belong to depths:** two or three kinds that only appear deep.
7. Open from earlier plans: DODGE and GLASS balance, Highway's single obstacle set, gamepad
   support, live mode capturing one app.

## Progress (2026-11-15, branch `feat/depth-run`)
All eight rules are built.
- `game.rs`: `World::depth`, the Heart's clock (`heart_t0`, `heart_due`, `set_heart_window`,
  `heart_clock`, `dive_now`), `break_heart` (the dive and its rewards), `heart_returns` (the
  next Heart or the victory lap), `heart_escapes`, `heart_hp`, `sync_mods`, `free_upgrade`.
  `enter_purgatory`, `encore` and all rage growth are gone; `HeartPhase::floor` is always 0 and
  `share` sizes a section's nodes. Depth replaces the song index in pressure, enemy health,
  elite kinds, modifiers and the arena's depth frames.
- `main.rs`: the song (or the DROP RUSH section) is passed as the Heart's clock; the offer at
  track clear is removed; results, run setup's BEST and the records lead with depth; rank is
  depths per track; milestones ask for depth 5 and 10.
- `render.rs`: the clock under the Heart's bar with its +3 and +2 marks and "DIVE +n", DEPTH
  beside the BPM, purgatory text that says whether another Heart is coming.
- `tools.rs`: `--sim` prints every dive of a run and, per tier, how the first Heart went and
  depths per song. `--song-idx N` now starts at depth 2N.
- Tests: `an_earlier_kill_dives_deeper_and_pays_more_per_depth`,
  `a_heart_that_is_never_broken_escapes_and_the_run_sinks_one_depth`,
  `a_new_heart_rises_after_purgatory_only_if_the_song_has_time_for_it`,
  `breaking_fast_never_makes_the_heart_or_the_pressure_grow`.

Numbers after tuning: `HEART_HP_PER_SEC` 1.9, Heart health +4% per depth (down from the 10%
first guessed: with it, kills slowed with depth whatever the build), Hearts up to 30% smaller
on the hardest tier (`HEART_TIER_EASE`), enemy health +15% per depth, pressure +10% per depth.
On PULSE the plain bot's first Heart escaped 2 times in 8, gave +1 four times and +2 twice.
With six damage upgrades at depth 6 it gave +2 every time, with a second dive after it.

**Not verified by a human:** whether the clock reads at a glance, whether +3 is reachable by a
person, how the purgatory and the new Heart feel, and the whole slope past depth 6. The gentle
tiers dive very fast in the simulator (5.5 depths a song on RESTING), which may be too much.
